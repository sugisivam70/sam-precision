Quality Assurance

Quality at Every Stage.

SAM PRECISION emphasizes manufacturing consistency, inspection, skilled workmanship, and continuous improvement to meet customer requirements. Our in-house inspection facility is equipped with standard measuring instruments to ensure strict dimensional adherence.

Digital Vernier Calipers

Precision measurement for external, internal, and depth dimensions across machined components.

Micrometers

High-resolution measurement for shaft diameters, thicknesses, and close-tolerance turned parts.

Height Gauges

Accurate height and step dimension verification on surface plates for complex fixtures and components.

Bore Gauges

Internal diameter, bore geometry, and cylindricity inspection for PB bushes and hydraulic cylinders.

Bevel Protractors

Precise angular inspection for tapers, bevels, and angled surfaces on machine-building parts.

Slip Gauges

Gauge blocks providing reference standards for tool calibration and gap verification.
