About Us

Built on Quality.
Driven by Precision.

SAM PRECISION was established in January 2021 and operates in Peenya Industrial Area, Bengaluru, one of India's major machine-tool manufacturing hubs.

The company specializes in manufacturing precision-machined components, hydraulic cylinders, PB bushes, jigs, fixtures, and special machine-building components. Supported by a skilled workforce, CAD-supported office operations, manufacturing infrastructure, and in-house inspection facilities, SAM PRECISION focuses on consistent quality, reliable delivery, and customer satisfaction.
