Company Profile

Built on Quality. Driven by Precision.

SAM PRECISION was established in January 2021 and operates in Peenya Industrial Area, Bengaluru, one of India's major machine-tool manufacturing hubs.

The company specializes in manufacturing precision-machined components, hydraulic cylinders, PB bushes, jigs, fixtures, and special machine-building components.

Supported by a skilled workforce, CAD-supported office operations, manufacturing infrastructure, and in-house inspection facilities, SAM PRECISION focuses on consistent quality, reliable delivery, and customer satisfaction.

Our Journey

Milestones of Progress

A chronology of precision machining growth and verified achievements.

2021

Establishment & Operations

SAM PRECISION was founded in January 2021 in Peenya Industrial Area, Bengaluru, focusing on precision-machined components and custom machine parts for industrial OEMs.

2023

Machinery & Infrastructure Expansion

Equipped an owned facility of approx. 2,500 sq. ft. with VMC (Vertical Machining Centre), CNC turning, BFW/HMT milling, and cylindrical/surface grinding machinery.

2025

ISO 9001:2015 & Supplier Recognition

Certified to ISO 9001:2015 for Quality Management Systems. Conferred the Best Quality Supplier Recognition Award for FY 2025–26 by Micromatic Grinding Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
