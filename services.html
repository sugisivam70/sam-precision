Manufacturing Flow

Precision Manufacturing Process

A structured workflow from CAD drawing verification to final quality inspection and delivery.

01

Drawing & CAD Verification

Detailed evaluation of client blueprints, geometric tolerances, material grades, and specifications using CAD-supported office systems.

02

Machining Setup & Programming

Setting up CNC turning, VMC, milling, or grinding operations according to component geometry and required dimensional accuracy.

03

In-Process Inspection

Conducting intermediate dimensional verification using digital verniers, micrometers, and height gauges during machining cycles.

04

Precision Production

Executing precision machining runs with experienced operators, supporting both small-batch requirements and bulk volume production.

05

Final Quality Audit & Delivery

Complete final inspection under ISO 9001:2015 QMS standards, packaging, and dependable on-time delivery to the customer.
